§ 1.4 · IN CONTEXT

Twelve frameworks, three regimes, four levers.

Dalio's economic engine produces three classical outcomes when a long-term debt cycle tops: ugly deflationary, beautiful, or ugly inflationary. The path that obtains depends on how policymakers mix four levers — debt reduction, austerity, wealth transfer, debt monetization.

This page renders all twelve of his frameworks. We start with the deleveragings sub-model — the moment where the regime branches.

STAGE 01 · THE QUESTION
"The differences between deleveragings depend on the amounts and paces of (1) debt reduction, (2) austerity, (3) transferring wealth from the haves to the have-nots, and (4) debt monetization." RAY DALIO · IN-DEPTH LOOK AT DELEVERAGINGS · 2012
STAGE 02 · THE FOUR LEVERS

Each lever shrinks debt-to-income — but with different side effects.

01
Debt reduction
Defaults, restructurings, write-downs. Investors absorb the loss.
DEFLATIONARY · DEPRESSING
02
Austerity
Cut spending. Households + governments reduce consumption.
DEFLATIONARY · DEPRESSING
03
Wealth transfer
Tax the haves; redistribute to the have-nots. Dalio: rare in scale.
NEUTRAL · POLITICAL
04
Debt monetization
Central bank prints money, buys debt. Currency dilutes.
INFLATIONARY · STIMULATIVE
STAGE 03 · THE HISTORICAL DECOMPOSITION

Same four levers — radically different mixes.

The 1930–32 Great Depression was ugly deflationary: defaults dominated, printing stayed near zero. Hoover-era austerity squeezed further.

Then in 1933 Roosevelt devalued gold and printing rose to ~2% of GDP. Same country, same debt — but a beautiful reflation followed.

Japan 1990+ looks like 1933 on paper, but printing stayed at 0.8% — too small to overcome the deflation. Chronic ugly deflationary.

STAGE 04 · THE FORMULA

The beautiful gate: nominal growth must exceed nominal interest.

CORE IDENTITY
$$G_t \;=\; \mathrm{NGDP}_{yoy,t} \;-\; r_t$$
If $G_t > 0$ and $\Delta D_t < 0$, the deleveraging is beautiful. If $G_t < 0$ and $\Delta D_t > 0$, it's ugly deflationary.
U.S. 1930–32 · WORKED
1$G = -17.0\% - 3.4\% = -20.4\mathrm{pp}$
2$\Delta D = +97\mathrm{pp}$ (DebtGDP $155\% \to 252\%$)
3$\pi = 0.4\% + 0.4\% = 0.8\%$ — print intensity stayed low
REGIME · DEFLATIONARY
G < 0 and ΔD > 0 ⇒ ugly deflationary.
